# Santiago Castellanos

Mechanical engineering student working on equipment design, prototyping, and
manufacturing.

I design mechanical hardware and the fixtures and tooling that make it, and I
take projects through prototyping and fabrication rather than stopping at the
drawing. I am currently an Undergraduate Research Assistant in the University
of Alberta's Welding Engineering Lab. Previously, I was an Equipment
Engineering Intern at Tesla.

I publish tools for design and manufacturing problems I encounter so the
reasoning, measured results, and failure boundaries are open to inspection.

## Selected public work

- **[Surface Unfold](https://github.com/tlosanti/fsae-livery-tool)** -
  segmentation and isometric flattening of curved shells into flat,
  manufacturable panels.
  - Scope: results are measured on one Formula SAE bodywork set and do not
    establish performance on arbitrary vehicles or curved surfaces.
- **[Relief Forge Tools](https://github.com/tlosanti/relief-forge-tools)** -
  mesh reconstruction and fabrication-validation tooling for dimensionally
  scaled STL candidates.
  - Scope: topology checks establish a closed, coherently oriented solid; they
    do not establish printability or castability.
